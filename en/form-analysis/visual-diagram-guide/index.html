Forms 1-8: Foundation Level¶

Form 1: Beginning Form (Qi Shi)¶

Movement Sequence:

Key Points: - Duration: 12-16 seconds per repetition - Breathing: Inhale (arms rise) → Exhale (hands press) - Weight: Stays 50/50 throughout - Purpose: Develops peng jin (expansive force) and mental focus

Practice Recommendations: - 10-20 repetitions daily - Stand quietly in Wu Chi stance for 30-60 seconds before beginning - Use this form to center yourself before longer practice sessions

How to Use These Diagrams¶

  1. Download the .excalidraw files
  2. Open at excalidraw.com (no account needed)
  3. Zoom in to see movement details
  4. Print or display on tablet during practice
  5. Reference the detailed breakdown pages for full instructions

Training Progression¶

Week 1-4: Focus on Forms 1-8 (Foundation) - Practice Form 1 daily (10-20 reps) - Add one new form per week - Use diagrams to check your positioning

Week 5-12: Complete all 24 forms - Learn Forms 9-24 - Practice full sequence 2-3 times per week - Reference diagrams for complex transitions

Month 4+: Refine and deepen - Study martial applications - Work on smooth transitions - Use diagrams to identify areas needing attention

Video Resources¶

For dynamic movement demonstrations, refer to:

  • Master Amin Wu - 24 Form Back View Demonstration (shows proper alignment from behind)
  • Peter Chen - Front view demonstrations with detailed explanations
  • Local class videos - Side view for posture checking

Note: Diagrams show static positions. For smooth, continuous movement, practice slowly and continuously rather than stopping at each position.
