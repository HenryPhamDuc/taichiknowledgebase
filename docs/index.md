# Tai Chi Knowledge Base

Welcome to the comprehensive Tai Chi training resource, structured across three skill levels: **Foundation**, **Advanced**, and **Elite**.

## What You'll Find Here

This knowledge base organizes Tai Chi training materials into a progressive learning path:

### Foundation Level
Build solid fundamentals. Learn proper posture, weight transfer, breathing, and the basic movement patterns that everything else builds on.

### Advanced Level  
Refine your practice. Work with finer distinctions in energy flow, timing, and application. Start integrating form with function.

### Elite Level
Master-level material. Deep work with internal arts, martial applications, and the subtle body mechanics that separate good from great.

## Bilingual Content

All content is available in both **English** and **Vietnamese** (Tiếng Việt). Navigate using the language selector to switch between versions.

## How to Use This Site

1. **Start at your level** — Don't skip Foundation material even if you're experienced. The basics reveal new depth each time you revisit them.
2. **Watch the videos** — Each section links to curated YouTube tutorials from respected instructors.
3. **Practice deliberately** — Read/watch, then practice away from the screen. Come back with questions.
4. **Cross-reference** — Use the deep dives to explore specific topics in detail.
