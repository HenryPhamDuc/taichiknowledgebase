# Foundation Level

This is where every Tai Chi journey begins — and where masters return to throughout their practice.

## Why Start Here?

Foundation material covers the non-negotiable basics: posture, alignment, weight transfer, breathing, and the fundamental movement patterns. These aren't "beginner topics" you graduate from — they're the ground you stand on for everything that follows.

I get asked all the time: *"I've been practicing for 5 years, do I still need to review basics?"* The answer is always yes. Not because you're doing it wrong, but because basics reveal new layers as your body learns to feel them.

## What's in This Section

### Basics
Core fundamentals taught through simple, repeatable exercises:
- Stance and posture (Wuji, Bow stance, Cat stance)
- Weight transfer and stepping
- Hand forms and basic arm positions
- Breathing coordination
- Single movements (Grasp Bird's Tail, Wave Hands Like Clouds, etc.)

### Deep Dives
Focused exploration of specific foundation topics:
- The 10 Essentials of Tai Chi (detailed breakdown)
- Yang Cheng Fu's principles
- Common mistakes and how to fix them
- Biomechanics of Tai Chi movement
- Building a daily practice routine

## How to Approach This Material

**Don't rush.** Foundation is not a checkbox. Spend weeks or months here, not days.

**Film yourself.** What you *think* you're doing and what you're *actually* doing are often different. Phone camera, 30 seconds, compare to the video.

**One thing at a time.** Don't try to fix posture, breathing, and weight transfer in the same session. Pick one focus per practice.

**Return often.** Even when you move to Advanced material, come back to Foundation videos monthly. You'll catch things you missed.
