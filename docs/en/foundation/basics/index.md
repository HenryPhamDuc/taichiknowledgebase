# Foundation Basics

Core fundamentals for building your Tai Chi practice from the ground up.

## Recommended Starting Sequence

Watch these in order, then practice each before moving to the next:

### 1. Introduction to Tai Chi
- **Tai Chi for Beginners (Lesson 1: Basic Training)** — Peter Chen (8:43)
  - Basic stance, posture, and weight distribution
  - [Watch on YouTube](https://youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID)

### 2. Stance Work
- **Wuji Stance** — Standing meditation and proper alignment
- **Bow Stance (Gong Bu)** — Weight distribution, knee alignment
- **Cat Stance (Xu Bu)** — Light stepping, balance
- **Empty Stance** — Preparation for movement

### 3. Weight Transfer
- **Shift and Step** — Moving from solid base
- **Rock and Roll** — Feeling weight travel through feet
- **Single Leg Balance** — Building stability

### 4. Basic Arm Movements  
- **Peng (Ward Off)** — Expansive energy
- **Lu (Rollback)** — Redirecting force
- **Ji (Press)** — Forward intention
- **An (Push)** — Grounded release

### 5. Single Movements
Start with these foundational patterns:
- **Grasp Bird's Tail** (right and left)
- **Wave Hands Like Clouds**
- **Single Whip**
- **White Crane Spreads Wings**
- **Brush Knee and Push**

## Practice Guidelines

**Frequency over duration:** 10-15 minutes daily beats 90 minutes once a week.

**Slow is smooth, smooth is fast:** Move slowly enough to feel what's happening. Speed comes later.

**One correction at a time:** Don't try to fix everything in one session. Today: posture. Tomorrow: breathing. Next week: weight transfer.

**Film yourself monthly:** Record 30 seconds of a basic movement. Compare to the instruction videos. Note one thing to improve.
