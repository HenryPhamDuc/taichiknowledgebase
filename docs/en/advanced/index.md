# Advanced Level

Refine your practice. Work with finer distinctions in energy flow, timing, and application.

## What Makes This "Advanced"?

At this level, you're no longer learning *what* to do — you're refining *how* you do it. The movements are familiar. Now we work with:

- **Energy flow (Qi)** — Feeling and directing internal movement
- **Timing and rhythm** — When to be fast/slow, heavy/light
- **Application** — Martial meaning behind the form
- **Integration** — Connecting form with function

## Prerequisites

Before diving into Advanced material:
- Comfortable with all Foundation basics
- Can demonstrate proper posture without thinking
- Have established a daily practice habit (even 10 minutes)
- Understand the 10 Essentials at an intellectual level
