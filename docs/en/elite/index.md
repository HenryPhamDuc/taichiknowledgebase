# Elite Level

Master-level material. Deep work with internal arts, martial applications, and the subtle body mechanics that separate good from great.

## What Defines Elite Practice?

Elite isn't about knowing more forms. It's about:

- **Subtlety** — Micro-adjustments that transform the movement
- **Internal power** — Fajin, silk reeling, whole-body connection
- **Martial effectiveness** — Application under pressure
- **Teaching ability** — Can you break it down for others?

## Prerequisites

Elite work assumes:
- Years of consistent Foundation and Advanced practice
- Comfortable with at least one complete form
- Have studied push hands with a partner
- Understand the difference between "doing Tai Chi" and "being in Tai Chi"
