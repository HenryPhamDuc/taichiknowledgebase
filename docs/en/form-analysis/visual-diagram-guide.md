## Forms 1-8: Foundation Level

### Form 1: Beginning Form (Qi Shi)

**Movement Sequence:**

![Form 1 Diagram](./diagrams/form-01-beginning.excalidraw)

**Key Points:**
- **Duration:** 12-16 seconds per repetition
- **Breathing:** Inhale (arms rise) → Exhale (hands press)
- **Weight:** Stays 50/50 throughout
- **Purpose:** Develops peng jin (expansive force) and mental focus

**Practice Recommendations:**
- 10-20 repetitions daily
- Stand quietly in Wu Chi stance for 30-60 seconds before beginning
- Use this form to center yourself before longer practice sessions

## How to Use These Diagrams

2. **Open** at [excalidraw.com](https://excalidraw.com) (no account needed)
3. **Zoom** in to see movement details
4. **Print** or display on tablet during practice
5. **Reference** the detailed breakdown pages for full instructions

---

## Training Progression

**Week 1-4:** Focus on Forms 1-8 (Foundation)
- Practice Form 1 daily (10-20 reps)
- Add one new form per week
- Use diagrams to check your positioning

**Week 5-12:** Complete all 24 forms
- Learn Forms 9-24
- Practice full sequence 2-3 times per week
- Reference diagrams for complex transitions

**Month 4+:** Refine and deepen
- Study martial applications
- Work on smooth transitions
- Use diagrams to identify areas needing attention
